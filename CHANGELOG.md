# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-17)

Right-click a game and choose Refresh metadata to fetch its title, art, platforms and achievements from its store again. A New filter shows the games that appeared in your library in the last 15 days. Newer Steam games now get their real covers, and a game with no art at all gets a Showcase cover with its title. Stats shows your achievements as a grid of games: open one for its achievements as cards, rarest first. Also: an Installed filter, Add game picks a platform from your signed-in stores, the sync menu lists only signed-in stores, calmer sync status and smoother filtering. Xbox is held back for now: Xbox Live stopped accepting the sign-in Showcase used.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-06)

Showcase now keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, it offers to install it (About has Check for updates). Also: platform logos throughout, physical copies counted with their console's platform, glossy posters, smarter matching of the same game across stores, a screenshot viewer, and your unlocked achievements per game on the Stats page.
